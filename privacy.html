Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

Information We Collect

Local Storage Data

The ATS Resume Builder stores your resume content, cover letter drafts, and user preferences locally in your browser using localStorage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device or browser
  • Is not transmitted to our servers
  • Can be cleared at any time through browser settings
  • Enables autosave functionality for your convenience

Analytics Data

We may use Google Analytics to understand how our platform is used. This includes:

  • Pages visited and time spent on features
  • Browser type and device information
  • General geographic location (country/region level)
  • Anonymous usage patterns and feature interactions

This data is aggregated and anonymized - we never link it to individual users or resumes.

Advertising Data

When Google AdSense is enabled, they may:

  • Use cookies to show relevant ads based on your interests
  • Collect information about ad interactions
  • Link data across websites for ad personalization

How We Use Your Information

Local Data

Your resume data is used exclusively for:

  • Providing the resume building service
  • Enabling autosave and offline functionality
  • Generating ATS compatibility scores
  • Creating PDF exports

Analytics

We use analytics data to:

  • Improve user experience and interface design
  • Identify popular features and usage patterns
  • Fix bugs and performance issues
  • Make informed decisions about new features

Data Sharing and Third Parties

Google Analytics

We share anonymous analytics data with Google Analytics under their privacy policy. Google may use this data to improve their services.

Google AdSense

AdSense partners may collect data for advertising purposes as outlined in their privacy policies.

No Resume Data Sharing

We never sell, share, or transmit your resume content to third parties. Your personal and professional information remains private on your device.

Data Security

Local Storage Security

Since your data is stored locally in your browser:

  • It's protected by your browser's security features
  • Not vulnerable to server breaches
  • Accessible only through your device and browser

HTTPS Encryption

All connections to our website use HTTPS encryption to protect data in transit.

Your Rights and Choices

Local Data Control

You have complete control over your locally stored data:

  • Clear data through browser settings
  • Delete individual resumes or all data
  • Export your data at any time
  • Disable local storage in browser settings

Analytics Opt-Out

You can opt out of Google Analytics by:

  • Installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser extension
  • Using browser privacy settings to block analytics

Advertising Choices

You can control ad personalization through:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Browser cookie preferences
  • Ad-blocking extensions

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International Data Transfers

Since your data is stored locally in your browser, no international data transfers occur for your resume content. Analytics and advertising data may be processed by third parties according to their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date. Significant changes will be highlighted on our website.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@ats-resume-builder.com
  • Website: https://ats-resume-builder.com/contact

GDPR Rights

For EU users, since your data is stored locally in your browser, you maintain full control. You can exercise your rights by clearing browser data or using the controls mentioned above.
