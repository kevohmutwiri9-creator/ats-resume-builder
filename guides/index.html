How to use these resources

These pages provide general writing and formatting guidance, not advice from a particular employer or a test against a specific hiring platform. Applicant-tracking systems vary, and employers can configure them differently.

Use the examples as starting points, not claims to copy. Replace sample details with your own experience, verify every skill and result, and follow the instructions in the job posting. The checker on this site estimates keyword overlap; it does not reproduce an employer's screening rules or predict an outcome.

A practical review before you apply

  1. Use familiar section headings such as Experience, Education, and Skills.
  2. Make your dates, job titles, and contact details easy to find.
  3. Compare your resume with the posting and add only accurate, relevant terminology.
  4. Read the exported document as plain text to catch missing or reordered content.
  5. Follow the employer's requested file type and application instructions.
