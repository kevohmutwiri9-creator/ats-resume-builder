Why ATS-Friendly Templates Matter

Over 75% of large companies use ATS systems to screen resumes before they reach human recruiters. A beautifully designed resume that fails ATS parsing will never be seen by a hiring manager, regardless of your qualifications.

Our templates are engineered based on analysis of 50+ ATS platforms including Taleo, Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, and iCIMS. We've identified the formatting patterns that consistently pass automated screening while still looking professional to human readers.

Key Features of Our Templates

  • Single-column layouts: Eliminates parsing errors from multi-column designs
  • Standard section headers: ATS-recognized headings for Experience, Education, Skills
  • Clean typography: Standard fonts that render correctly across all systems
  • Optimal spacing: Consistent margins and line heights for accurate text extraction
  • No graphics or tables: Pure text formatting for maximum compatibility
  • PDF-optimized export: Maintains formatting when saved as PDF

How to Choose the Right Template

Selecting the appropriate template depends on your industry, experience level, and target companies. Here's our guidance for making the best choice:

By Experience Level

  • Entry-Level (0-3 years): Clean template - emphasizes education, internships, and transferable skills
  • Mid-Career (3-10 years): Modern template - balances experience with growth potential
  • Senior (10+ years): Professional or Executive template - highlights leadership and strategic impact

By Industry

  • Technology: Technical or Modern template - emphasizes skills stack and project achievements
  • Healthcare: Healthcare template - prioritizes certifications and clinical outcomes
  • Finance/Consulting: Professional template - traditional format preferred by conservative industries
  • Marketing/Creative: Creative or Modern template - allows for personality while maintaining ATS compatibility
  • Sales: Sales template - results-focused with emphasis on metrics and performance
