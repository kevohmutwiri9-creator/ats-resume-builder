Resume Layouts

Before you submit a resume

These layouts have not been validated against every employer's applicant-tracking system. Systems and employer settings vary, so a template cannot guarantee successful parsing, an interview, or a job offer.

  1. Use the layout that presents your experience most clearly.
  2. Check that your name, contact details, section headings, and dates are readable.
  3. Export or print the document, then review every page for missing or misplaced text.
  4. Where possible, copy the document text into a plain-text editor and check its reading order.
  5. Follow the file format and application instructions in the job posting.

The keyword checker estimates text overlap with a job description. It is not an ATS compatibility test.
