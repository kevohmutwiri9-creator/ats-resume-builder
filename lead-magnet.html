Free ATS Optimization Checklist

Use this checklist to make sure your resume passes applicant tracking systems and reaches a real recruiter.

  1. Use standard headings: Experience, Education, Skills.
  2. Avoid images, text boxes, headers/footers that break parsing.
  3. Include role-specific keywords from the job description.
  4. Use simple bullets, avoid tables and multi-column layouts.
  5. Save and export as PDF using the builder's export feature.
  6. Use conventional section ordering and clear dates.
  7. Limit use of special characters and emojis.
