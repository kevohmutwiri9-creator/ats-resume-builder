Why This Checklist Matters

Over 75% of large companies use ATS systems to screen resumes before they reach human recruiters. A resume that fails ATS parsing will never be seen by a hiring manager, regardless of your qualifications. This checklist helps you avoid common mistakes and optimize your resume for automated screening.

The ATS Challenge

ATS systems use sophisticated algorithms to parse, analyze, and rank resumes. They look for specific patterns, keywords, and formatting conventions. When your resume doesn't meet these criteria, it's often automatically rejected before a human ever sees it. This checklist is designed to help you navigate these technical requirements.

Formatting Essentials

Proper formatting is the foundation of ATS compatibility. Follow these rules to ensure your resume can be accurately parsed.

Layout and Structure

  • ✅ Use single-column layout - multi-column layouts confuse parsers
  • ✅ Standard section headings: Experience, Education, Skills, Summary
  • ✅ Consistent spacing throughout the document
  • ✅ Minimum 0.5 inch margins on all sides
  • ❌ Avoid tables for layout purposes
  • ❌ No text boxes or embedded graphics
  • ❌ Skip headers and footers - content often missed
  • ❌ Avoid two-column designs

Typography and Fonts

  • ✅ Use standard fonts: Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, Georgia, Helvetica
  • ✅ Font size: 10-12pt for body, 14-16pt for headers
  • ✅ Consistent font usage throughout
  • ❌ Avoid decorative or custom fonts
  • ❌ No script fonts or unusual typefaces
  • ❌ Limit use of special characters and symbols

Date Formats

  • ✅ Use consistent format: "Month Year" or "MM/YYYY"
  • ✅ Examples: "January 2020" or "01/2020"
  • ❌ Avoid: "01/20", "Jan '20", "2020-01"
  • ❌ Don't mix date formats in the same resume

Content Optimization

Beyond formatting, your content must be strategically written to perform well in ATS scoring.

Keyword Strategy

  • ✅ Extract keywords from job descriptions
  • ✅ Include industry-standard terminology
  • ✅ Use both acronyms and full terms (e.g., "SEO" and "Search Engine Optimization")
  • ✅ Place keywords in summary, skills, and experience sections
  • ❌ Avoid keyword stuffing - only include relevant skills
  • ❌ Don't create isolated keyword lists

Achievement-Focused Writing

  • ✅ Use action verbs to start bullet points
  • ✅ Include quantifiable metrics and results
  • ✅ Formula: Action + What + How + Measurable Result
  • ✅ Examples: "Increased revenue by 34%", "Reduced costs by $50K"
  • ❌ Avoid vague statements like "responsible for"
  • ❌ Don't list responsibilities without outcomes

Section Best Practices

  • ✅ Contact info in body, not headers/footers
  • ✅ Professional summary (2-3 sentences) at top
  • ✅ Skills section with categorized abilities
  • ✅ Work experience in reverse chronological order
  • ✅ Education with degrees, institutions, dates
  • ❌ No photos or personal information
  • ❌ Avoid references section (save for later)

Common ATS Mistakes to Avoid

These are the most frequent reasons resumes fail ATS screening. Check your resume against this list before submitting.

Technical Errors

  • ❌ Complex tables and nested layouts
  • ❌ Graphics, logos, or images within the resume
  • ❌ Non-standard file formats (use PDF or DOCX)
  • ❌ Encrypted or password-protected files
  • ❌ Scanned documents or image-only PDFs

Content Issues

  • ❌ Missing contact information
  • ❌ Inconsistent section headings
  • ❌ Vague or generic descriptions
  • ❌ Spelling and grammatical errors
  • ❌ Overuse of buzzwords without substance

Formatting Problems

  • ❌ Inconsistent bullet point styles
  • ❌ Excessive use of bold, italics, or underlining
  • ❌ Inconsistent capitalization
  • ❌ Improper use of special characters
  • ❌ Poor spacing and alignment

Final Verification Steps

Before submitting your resume, complete these final checks to ensure ATS compatibility.

Export Test

  1. Export your resume to PDF
  2. Copy-paste the PDF text into a plain text editor
  3. Verify text remains readable and properly formatted
  4. Check that no characters are scrambled or missing

ATS Score Check

  1. Use our ATS Checker tool
  2. Upload your resume and a job description
  3. Review your ATS compatibility score
  4. Address any identified issues before applying

Human Review

  1. Have a colleague review your resume
  2. Check for clarity and readability
  3. Verify all information is accurate
  4. Ensure formatting looks professional
