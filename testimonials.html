Using resume examples responsibly

Examples can help you understand structure and wording, but they are not a substitute for your own work history. The sample resumes and bullets on this site are illustrative; do not present sample employers, qualifications, or results as your own.

About job-search outcomes

Interview and hiring outcomes depend on many factors, including the role, employer, timing, and a candidate's qualifications. This site does not publish independently verified customer outcome data, so it does not promise a particular response rate, interview, or job offer.

Make an example your own

  1. Keep the structure that helps a reader understand your experience.
  2. Replace every sample detail with information you can verify.
  3. Use numbers only when you can explain their source and context.
  4. Tailor wording to the role without claiming skills or experience you do not have.
