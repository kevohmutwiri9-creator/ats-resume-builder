About ATS Resume Builder

A practical set of job-application tools

ATS Resume Builder is a browser-based project for drafting a resume, preparing a cover letter, and reviewing resume text against a job description. The goal is to make common editing steps available without requiring an account.

What the tools do

  • Resume builder: Enter contact details, a summary, work experience, education, and skills; choose a layout; and review a print-ready preview.
  • Keyword checker: Compare pasted resume text with a job description, show likely matching and missing terms, and run a small set of text checks.
  • Cover letter builder: Draft and edit a cover letter in the browser.
  • Guides and examples: Review general writing suggestions and illustrative examples, then adapt them to your real experience.

How to interpret a match score

The checker uses text matching and a built-in list of common phrases to estimate overlap. It does not connect to an employer's applicant-tracking system, know that employer's screening settings, or predict an interview or job offer. A high percentage is not a guarantee. Add a suggested term only when it truthfully describes your qualifications.

Your drafts and privacy

Resume drafts are saved in local storage in the browser you use. The checker compares the text in the page. If you clear browser site data or switch devices, locally saved drafts may no longer be available. Review the Privacy Policy for information about analytics, advertising, and other site technologies.
