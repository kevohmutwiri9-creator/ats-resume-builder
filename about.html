About ATS Resume Builder

Our Mission

ATS Resume Builder was created to help job seekers navigate the modern recruitment landscape. With over 75% of large companies using Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to screen resumes, we recognized a critical need for a tool that helps candidates create resumes that actually get seen by human recruiters.

Why ATS-Friendly Matters

Most resumes never reach human eyes because they fail ATS screening. Common issues include complex formatting, graphics, and keyword mismatches. Our tool eliminates these barriers by providing ATS-optimized templates and real-time feedback to ensure your resume passes automated screening and reaches hiring managers.

What We Offer

  • Professional Templates: Clean, modern designs tested for ATS compatibility
  • Real-time Optimization: Instant feedback on ATS score and keyword matching
  • AI-Powered Suggestions: Smart content recommendations to strengthen your resume
  • PDF Export: ATS-safe formatting with one-click download
  • Cover Letter Builder: Coordinated application materials
  • Local Storage: Your data stays private and secure in your browser

Our Commitment to Quality

We continuously update our templates and algorithms based on real ATS data and hiring trends. Our team includes HR professionals, recruiters, and career coaches who ensure our recommendations reflect current best practices in resume writing and job application strategies.

Success Stories

Thousands of job seekers have used our platform to land interviews at top companies. Users report 3x higher response rates when using ATS-optimized resumes compared to traditional formats.
