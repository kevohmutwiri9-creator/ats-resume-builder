Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a bug?

Use the contact form above and select "Bug Report" as the subject. Please include details about your browser, operating system, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots are helpful when available.

Can I request new features?

Absolutely! We love hearing from our users about features that would help them succeed. Submit your ideas through the contact form, and our product team reviews all requests weekly.

Is there a mobile app available?

Our website is fully responsive and works great on mobile devices. We're exploring native app development based on user demand - let us know if you'd be interested!

How can I provide feedback?

We welcome all feedback! Use the contact form with "Feedback & Suggestions" selected, or email us directly at feedback@ats-resume-builder.com.
